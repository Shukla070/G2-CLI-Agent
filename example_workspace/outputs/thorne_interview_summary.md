# Summary: Interview with Dr. Aris Thorne
**Topic:** Havenport Coastal Erosion

## Key Points

**Seawall Failure**
The Havenport seawall, designed in the 1980s using historical (static) weather data, is deteriorating faster than projected. Rising and warming seas have increased the kinetic energy of routine winter storms, subjecting the structure to forces beyond its original design tolerances.

**Structural Deterioration**
Saltwater has infiltrated micro-fractures in the retaining blocks, corroding the internal steel rebar. As the rebar rusts and expands, it causes the concrete to break apart from the inside out — a compounding failure. Dr. Thorne warns that a major storm surge could cause physical collapse, directing ocean water directly into Havenport's lower residential basin.

**Recommended Long-Term Solutions**
Dr. Thorne advocates for managed retreat from vulnerable coastlines and the restoration of salt marshes, which serve as natural shock absorbers. He argues this approach is both more sustainable and more cost-effective than continuing to build higher concrete barriers.

**Immediate Priorities**
In the near term, Dr. Thorne recommends continuous structural monitoring, targeted reinforcement of the most vulnerable wall sections, and improved flood preparedness for nearby communities.
