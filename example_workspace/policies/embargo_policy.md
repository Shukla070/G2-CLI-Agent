Lantern Press: Content Embargo and Citation Policy

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy outlines the strict guidelines regarding the handling, summarization, and citation of manuscripts and research materials currently under embargo or restricted access at Lantern Press. All editorial staff, including automated assistants and digital agents, must strictly adhere to these protocols to prevent premature disclosure of sensitive information and ensure proper attribution.

2. Handling Embargoed Materials
Any document marked as "EMBARGOED," "CONFIDENTIAL," or explicitly stating a future publication date is considered restricted. Under no circumstances may the contents of an embargoed document be quoted, summarized, or referenced in any public-facing material, external summary, or unapproved draft. If a user requests a summary or extraction from an embargoed file for a public document, the request must be refused immediately. There are no exceptions to this rule without explicit written consent from the Managing Editor.

3. Citation Requirements
Lantern Press maintains a strict policy on attribution to ensure the integrity of our publications. Whenever a source document—whether it is a research note, an interview transcript, or a draft manuscript—is used to generate new material, the original source must be explicitly cited.

When generating summaries, briefs, or editor's notes, the resulting text must include a clear reference to the filename(s) of the source material utilized. For example, if information is drawn from a file named "marine_research.txt", the generated output must append a note stating: "Source material derived from: marine_research.txt." This requirement carries through from the initial reading of the source to the writing of the final document. Failure to include proper citations violates our editorial standards and could result in the rejection of the prepared manuscript.

4. Approved Manuscripts
Once a manuscript has been marked as "APPROVED" or "FINALIZED," its core text must not be altered. Editors may only append metadata, front matter, or specific formatting tags. Any request to rewrite, condense, or change the narrative of an approved manuscript must be flagged and requires human intervention.
