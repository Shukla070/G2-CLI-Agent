**Marketing and Editorial Sync - November**
**Attendees:** Sarah Jenkins (Managing Editor), Liam Foster (Head of Marketing), Marcus Vance (Senior Editor)

### Agenda Item 1: Promotional Strategy for *The Silent Ocean*

Liam opened the meeting by presenting the preliminary marketing rollout for *The Silent Ocean*. The strategy relies heavily on a digital campaign emphasizing the high-resolution photography captured during the Mariana Trench expedition. Liam requested that the editorial team prepare a series of three short, engaging blog posts summarizing the key discoveries from the book. These posts will be used to drive pre-orders and build audience interest ahead of the official launch. Marcus agreed to have the drafts ready by Friday, noting that he will use the raw research notes on the Yeti Crab as the primary source material while ensuring the content remains accessible to a general audience.

### Agenda Item 2: Addressing Embargoed Content

Sarah interjected to remind Liam that certain sections of the upcoming catalog, specifically the chapters detailing the controversial climate modeling predictions for the next decade, are strictly under embargo until January 1st. Liam acknowledged this and confirmed that the marketing team has updated their internal tracking to ensure no promotional material touches on those topics. Sarah also noted that the new Lantern AI system is programmed to refuse any summarization requests involving these files, reducing the risk of accidental disclosure before the embargo is lifted.

### Agenda Item 3: Audience Engagement Metrics

The team reviewed the engagement metrics from the previous quarter. Long-form articles focusing on practical environmental solutions saw a 40% increase in readership compared to purely theoretical pieces. Based on this data, Marcus proposed shifting the editorial focus of the upcoming Q2 releases to highlight actionable urban planning and adaptive management strategies, tying directly into the recent coastal erosion research. Liam supported this pivot, noting that "solutions journalism" is currently highly marketable and aligns well with audience interests. The meeting concluded with a plan to draft a revised editorial calendar by next Tuesday, incorporating the proposed changes and updated publication priorities.
